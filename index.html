Everything runs in your browser — add the people in your household, log income and outgoings at whatever frequency they occur, and the planner routes money into your accounts and splits the rest.

Sandbox: you're viewing a scenario — changes here never touch your live data.

Accounts

Create the pots your money should flow into — a shared account, tax set-aside, pension, savings — and give each one rules like “20% of Alex's income” or a fixed amount per week. The UK preset adds Income Tax and Class 4 NI pots with 2025/26 banded rates on profit — categorise deductible outgoings as “Business” so they're subtracted first. An estimate, not tax advice.
