Income & Shared Budget Planner

Pool incomes at any pay frequency, route money into the accounts you define, and split what's left the way that suits you.

New here?

Everything runs in your browser — add the people in your household, log income and outgoings at whatever frequency they occur, and the planner routes money into your accounts and splits the rest.

Accounts

Create the pots your money should flow into — a shared account, tax set-aside, pension, savings — and give each one rules like “20% of Alex's income” or a fixed amount per week.

Statistics & Snapshots

Totals above follow the view interval you pick (per day, week, month, or year). Capture snapshots to build a history and chart how things evolve.

Your data stays on this device. Everything is stored locally in your browser — nothing is sent to any server, and there are no accounts or tracking. Use “Export backup” to keep a copy outside the browser.
